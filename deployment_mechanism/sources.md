# Deployment Mechanism: Citations & Evidence Sources

1. **REXUS User Manual (RX_UserManual_v7-17_26Nov21)**
   - Sections 5.10.1, 5.10.2, 5.10.3: Sounding rocket ascent acceleration (20g axial), spin dynamics (3-4 Hz despun by yo-yo to 0.08 Hz), re-entry loads (20g shock, 8 m/s parachute landing impact).
   - Section 10.3: NSRHB vibration test qualification spectrum.

2. **Svarog ESA Proposal Form (RXBX17_SNSA-ESA_CALL_ExperimentProposalForm_18Jun25)**
   - Objectives & Mission Architecture: Deploy 4 composite booms from central spool cassette to 2.1 m nominal length (1 m minimum), deploy/observe/retract timeline (20-45 s deploy, 5 s dwell, 30-60 s retract).
   - Envelope: 4U electronics volume plus dedicated ~20x20x5 cm spool compartment.

3. **BEXUS 34 Flight Lessons & ESMATS 2025 Paper**
   - Documentation of steel tape deployment failure: tape blooming during dynamic loads causing booms to snag on door cutouts, achieving only ~0.5 m extension of 2.0 m intended length.
   - Core recommendation: Implement continuous anti-blooming roller constraints and guide throats.

4. **Fernandez, J. M. (2017) "Advanced Deployable Shell-Based Composite Booms" (NASA TM-2017-219569)**
   - Mechanics of deployable composite shell coiling, transition zones, anti-blooming constraint rollers, and deployer mechanism kinematics.

5. **Thomas's Obsidian Vault Reference: `Atlas/Projects/Svarog/Boom-Facts.md`**
   - Section 3: "Deployment mechanism and loads", synthesized from proposal docx, REXUS manual, and CAD geometry.

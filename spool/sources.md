# Spool Subsystem: Citations & Evidence Sources

1. **Project Svarog Sizing Analysis (`Composite Booms/Scripts/sizing.py` & `params.py`)**
   - Corrected coiling stress formulas, Budiansky-Argon microbuckling calculations, and radial buildup algorithms.

2. **Fernandez, J. M. (2017) "Advanced Deployable Shell-Based Composite Booms" (NASA TM-2017-219569)**
   - Formulae for coiling strains $\epsilon = t / (2 R_{hub})$, anticlastic curvature effects, and spool hub ratio design criteria for thin-walled composite lenticular and TRAC booms.

3. **Lee, A. J., & Fernandez, J. M. (2018) "Design and Analysis of Ultralight Deployable Booms" (AIAA Scitech)**
   - Analytical models of tape packaging, wrap buildup, and root boundary conditions for co-wound deployable booms.

4. **Budiansky, B. (1983) "Micromechanics" & Argon, A. S. (1972) "Fracture of Composites"**
   - The Budiansky-Argon criterion for compressive fiber microbuckling and matrix shear yielding limits.

5. **Thomas's Obsidian Vault Reference: `Atlas/Projects/Svarog/Boom-Facts.md`**
   - Section 2 (Geometry, layup, material) and Section 3 (Deployment mechanism and loads).

We're putting the half-millimetre aluminium sheet back on the blocks. Last week we dropped it because a vacuum bag at one bar couldn't press a badly formed sheet flat, and glued to wood it would tear itself off, since over the cure it grows about three quarters of a millimetre more than the plywood. The autoclave and the silicone plug now press five to ten times harder than the sheet needs, and we don't glue it: one screw in the middle, two in slots, release agent underneath, so it slides every cure. It has to be half-hard H14, because soft sheet would wrinkle. It also gives us the aluminium face Caltech cured on, and it seals the end grain. The only cost is cutting the wood half a millimetre smaller.

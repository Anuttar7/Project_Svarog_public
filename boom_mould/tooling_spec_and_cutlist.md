# Tooling Specification & Cut List: 500 mm Diagonal Boom Mould

## 1. Tool Assembly Architecture
The boom mould is designed as a split two-piece co-cure tool (Left Flange Tool and Right Flange Tool) clamped together along the central web plane:
- Each half-tool features a convex $90^\circ$ circular mandrel ($R = 15.10\text{ mm}$ nominal), a vertical web face ($10.0\text{ mm}$ height), and horizontal vacuum sealing lands ($25.0\text{ mm}$ width).
- When clamped together face-to-face, the two flanges are laid up and co-cured in a single oven cycle, forming a bonded monolithic TRAC section without secondary adhesive films.

## 3. High-Temperature Vacuum Bagging Schedule (Easy Composites Consumables)
1. **Release Treatment:**
   - 4 coats of Chemlease 15 Sealer followed by 5 coats of Frekote 700-NC release agent (baked $15\text{ min}$ at $100^\circ\text{C}$ between coats).
2. **Release Film:**
   - Perforated FEP Release Film (P1 release film, rated to $200^\circ\text{C}$).
3. **Breather / Bleeder:**
   - BR180 non-woven polyester breather cloth ($180\text{ g/m}^2$, rated to $205^\circ\text{C}$).
4. **Vacuum Bag Film:**
   - Easy Composites VB200 high-temperature nylon bagging film ($50\,\mu\text{m}$, rated to $204^\circ\text{C}$).
5. **Sealant Tape:**
   - AT-200Y high-temperature yellow mastic sealant tape (rated to $204^\circ\text{C}$).
6. **Through-Bag Vacuum Connector:**
   - TBC2 high-temperature steel through-bag connector with silicone high-temp o-rings.

## 4. Quality Control & Acceptance Checklist
- [ ] Tool straightness checked against Class 0 granite surface plate: camber $< 0.15\text{ mm}$ over $550\text{ mm}$.
- [ ] Vacuum drop test at room temperature: $< 10\text{ mbar}$ loss over $15\text{ minutes}$ at $950\text{ mbar}$ vacuum.
- [ ] Thermal soak trial at $180^\circ\text{C}$ for 2 hours with bare bagged tool: verify no bag leakage, no wood outgassing bubbles, and aluminium face sliding freely in slots.

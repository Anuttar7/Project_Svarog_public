## 2. Forming: pre-form over a former, then fit cold (review v5 #1, #2)
  formed over the wood R17.5 itself: O springs to R18.3, H14 to R21.4 [der]
  moment to hold H14 at R17.5 from that: 7.46 N mm/mm, about its full plastic moment [der]
  a lifted lip closes only over lambda = sqrt(2m/p); the gap left at its end is ~0.42 dkappa lambda^2:
    0.09 MPa: lip 12.9 mm, end gap 0.72 mm [der]
    0.40 MPa: lip  6.1 mm, end gap 0.16 mm [der]
    0.80 MPa: lip  4.3 mm, end gap 0.08 mm [der]
  So the bag cannot be trusted to seat it. Pre-form instead:
    H14 at proof 85 MPa: form over R15.5 to spring back to R17.5 (outer-fibre strain 1.6 %) [der]
    H14 at proof 120 MPa: form over R14.8 to spring back to R17.5 (outer-fibre strain 1.7 %) [der]
  Proof stress varies by coil, so: CNC one R14.8 former from ply offcut, form, check on the block,
  open by hand if tight. Accept when a 0.1 mm feeler will not enter anywhere along the arc. [der]

## 3. Thermal: the liner floats on one pin and must slide every cycle without yielding
  130 C cure: liner grows 0.75 mm more than the ply over 390; outer screws move 0.29 mm (3 mm slots) [der]
  150 C cure: liner grows 0.89 mm more than the ply over 390; outer screws move 0.34 mm (3 mm slots) [der]
  Friction collected over half the length (195 mm), summed across the developed width, against the force
  that yields the whole section. The web strip sees block pressure, the rest sees bag pressure. [der]
   gauge    mu  friction N  1050A-H14 N         5754-H22 N  web strip alone, N/mm vs H14 42.5
   3 bar  0.10         662         2226  slides    3405  slides   28.1
   3 bar  0.15         993         2226  slides    3405  slides   42.1
   3 bar  0.30        1986         2226  slides    3405  slides   84.2

   7 bar  0.15        1986         2226  slides    3405  slides   84.2
   7 bar  0.30        3972         2226  YIELDS    3405  YIELDS  168.5
  Reading: released, the section slides at 3 and 7 bar; the web strip alone is marginal at 7 bar,
  so put FEP film (0.05) under the liner on the web lands only. Dry, it yields: release is not optional.
  Before fitting the long liner, pull a 20 mm strip out from between two released, sealed ply faces
  under a known load at 130 C and read mu. If mu > 0.15, use 5754-H22 0.5 mm instead of 1050A. [der]
  Cooldown under pressure reverses the slip: the stress goes from +f to -f. That range of 2f stays
  elastic while f is below yield, which is the same test as the table (shakedown). [der]

## 4. What the liner does to the numbers that matter
  sheet thickness +-0.04: tool radius +-0.2 % against a spring-in bracket of +-13 %: noise [der]
  web gap: +-0.04 on each web land is +-0.08 on 0.40. Micrometer each liner at 3 points along
  its length, reject a spread above 0.02, and set the 0.36 stops between liner faces from the readings [der]
  tool-part friction: an aluminium face grows 0.23 % more than the laminate over the cure (review v5 #4).
  Whether that changes spring-in or waviness on a 0.2 mm part is not known; the bare coupon answers it. [der]

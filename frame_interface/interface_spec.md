# Frame Interface: Mechanical & Structural Specifications

## 1. Geometric Boundaries
- **Chassis Envelope:** $100.0\text{ mm} \times 100.0\text{ mm} \times 100.0\text{ mm}$ (1U structural unit); payload cassette module allocates a $200.0\text{ mm} \times 200.0\text{ mm} \times 50.0\text{ mm}$ volume in the 4U module.
- **Corner Rail Width:** $8.50\text{ mm}$ outer leg, $1.60\text{ mm}$ thickness.
- **Contact Pad Geometry:** $6.0\text{ mm} \times 6.0\text{ mm} \times 3.0\text{ mm}$ pad at each of the 8 frame corners.
- **Transverse Face Clear Aperture:** Face cutouts allow $35.0\text{ mm}$ horizontal $\times 12.0\text{ mm}$ vertical boom deployment clearances with minimum $2.0\text{ mm}$ surrounding perimeter buffer.

## 2. Structural Load Transfer
- **Ascent Axial Acceleration:** $20\text{g}$ axial load ($+Z$ axis):
  - Total deployer cassette mass: $\approx 1.20\text{ kg}$.
  - Quasi-static axial force: $F_z = 1.20\text{ kg} \times 20 \times 9.81\text{ m/s}^2 \approx 235.4\text{ N}$.
  - Reacted across 4 corner mounting hardpoints ($\approx 58.9\text{ N}$ shear/tension per hardpoint).
  - Safety Factor on 6061-T6 fasteners: $> 10.0$.
- **Ascent Lateral Acceleration:** $5\text{g}$ transverse load ($X/Y$ axes):
  - Quasi-static lateral force: $F_{xy} = 1.20\text{ kg} \times 5 \times 9.81\text{ m/s}^2 \approx 58.9\text{ N}$.
  - Reacted by shear pins and clamping friction at hardpoints.

## 3. Thermal & Material Compatibility
- **Frame Material:** Aluminium Alloy 6061-T6 (anodised or chem-filmed).
- **Fasteners:** A4-70 / 316 Stainless Steel screws with A286 lock washers or Helicoil inserts to prevent galling with aluminium threads.
- **Galvanic Isolation:** Direct contact between raw carbon composite booms and aluminium frame during stowed state is prevented by PTFE guide roller barriers to eliminate galvanic corrosion couples.

Last week's version dropped the sheet because a vacuum bag at one bar could not press a badly formed sheet flat, and glued aluminium over 390 mm fails as it grows about 0.75 mm more than plywood during the cure. The part now cures in an autoclave at 3 to 7 bar, with a silicone plug pressing every face. That is 5 to 10 times the pressure needed to flatten the sheet's springback onto the wood. One screw holds the middle, two more run through 3 mm slots, and release agent sits underneath, so it slides every cure and stays elastic. The Caltech group we copy cured on aluminium tools, so an aluminium face keeps us closer to them.
